Privacy Policy

Effective July 31, 2026

What this policy covers

This policy explains how the Goonsquad team app collects, uses, and protects information when team members and visitors use goonsquad.app.

Information we collect

  • Account details you provide, including your email address and username.
  • Profile information and requests to link your account to a team player record.
  • Saved plays, created content, feed posts, comments, reactions, and other activity.
  • Basic technical information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the app.

Authentication is handled by our authentication provider. The app does not store plaintext passwords.

Public team and social information

The app may display team schedules, results, statistics, and official Goonsquad social posts obtained from public or authorized sources. Social posts remain attributed and linked to their original platform.

How we use information

  • Provide accounts, player profiles, saved content, and member-only team features.
  • Operate the team feed and its posting, commenting, and reaction features.
  • Manage access to private plays and strategy content.
  • Protect the app, investigate problems, and improve the experience.

Service providers and sharing

We use service providers for hosting, authentication, and data storage. Information is shared with them only as needed to operate the app. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect the app and its users.

Retention and your choices

Information is retained while your account is active and as reasonably needed to operate the team service. You may ask a team administrator to correct your profile, unlink your player record, or delete your account and associated member content.

Security and age

We use reasonable safeguards and access controls, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. The app is intended for Goonsquad team members and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made. For questions or requests, contact a Goonsquad administrator through the app or the team's usual communication channel.
